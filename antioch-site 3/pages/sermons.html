在沈默中引導的上帝 God Who Guides in Silence
白恩拾牧師 · 2026年8月23日 Pastor Victor Pai · August 23, 2026
創世記 / Gen 24:1–33, 50–61
當上帝沈默時，祂仍在引導。透過亞伯拉罕僕人尋找利百加的旅程， 看見上帝在靜默中美麗的帶領。 When God is silent, He is still guiding. Through the journey of Abraham's servant seeking Rebekah, we see God's beautiful leadership even in stillness.
