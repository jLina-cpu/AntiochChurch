我們是誰Who We Are

溫哥華安提阿教會Antioch Church Vancouver

A Community of Faith信仰的群體

溫哥華安提阿教會是由中港台來的加拿大華人所組成的基督徒群體。這個大家庭的成員，在上帝眼中個個都是尊貴與獨特的。當我們生活在一起時，會學習去尊重並欣賞這些獨特的個體所帶來的差異與多樣性。

感謝主，人們的獨特性雖多而不同，但基督的福音把我們緊緊的連結在一起，成為完整的身體。因著愛的承載力，教會成為我們的方舟，而在方舟裡，我們成為一個生命共同體——共享上帝所賜永恆的生命。 Vancouver Antioch Church is a Christian community formed by Chinese Canadians from mainland China, Hong Kong, and Taiwan. Each person here is precious and unique in God's eyes. As we live together, we learn to respect and appreciate the diversity each individual brings.

Christ's gospel binds us together into one body. Through the power of love, the church becomes our ark — and within it, we share in the eternal life given by God.

「上帝愛世人」，說出了基督信仰最獨特的體驗。 "God so loved the world" — this captures the most distinctive experience of the Christian faith.

Three Foundations三個立會根基

向華人宣教Mission to the Chinese Community
本教會由在維真學院進修神學的傳道人、台灣中華福音神學院的牧者及卑詩大學的學生共同創立。我們珍惜每一個中國靈魂的價值，盼望他們飽嘗上帝的慈愛，在今生有高貴、榮耀、自由的生活。 Our Mandarin-speaking church was founded by pastors from Regent College, the China Evangelical Seminary of Taipei, and UBC graduate students. We cherish the value of every Chinese soul and hope they would come to know Jesus Christ personally.

解經與神學Scriptural Teaching & Theology
我們是持守正統信仰的福音派教會，不隸屬任何教派。我們強調正確的解經原理，並在講道事奉中整合神學研究，盼望以準確的聖經註釋為基礎，發展出合於華人處境的應用神學與倫理學。 We are an evangelical church upholding orthodox Protestant doctrine. We emphasise sound biblical exegesis and theological research, hoping to develop applied theology and ethics suited for contemporary Chinese Christians.
🕊️
聖靈的恩賜The Gifts of the Holy Spirit
我們對各種聖靈恩賜保持尊重的態度。按照聖經原則與教會歷史的經驗，查驗每一種恩賜是否出於聖靈。我們相信聖靈繼續賜下各樣恩賜，掌管並潔淨二十一世紀基督的教會。 We hold a respectful attitude toward all spiritual gifts, testing each by biblical principles and church tradition. We believe the Holy Spirit continues to give gifts today, leading and cleansing the Church in the 21st century.
名字的意義Why "Antioch"?

為什麼叫「安提阿教會」？The Story Behind the Name

Acts 11:19–30使徒行傳 11:19–30

「安提阿」是古代敘利亞的一個大都市。根據聖經使徒行傳第11章19–30節的記載，安提阿教會是早期基督教教會，在以色列以外的地方，首先傳播建立的教會之一。

這個教會也成為使徒保羅個人信仰成長的家，以及外出宣教的大本營。他們重視宣教工作、強調聖經教導，並接受聖靈的恩賜服事。

就是這樣一個模範的教會，成為我們學習效法的對象。溫哥華的安提阿教會，亦希望以向普世華人宣教、重視解經傳道、尊重聖靈的恩賜與工作，作為建立教會最基本的原則。 "Antioch" was a major city in ancient Syria. According to Acts 11:19–30, the Antioch church was one of the earliest Christian communities established outside Jewish Palestine. It was also the spiritual home of the apostle Paul and his missionary base.

The ancient Antioch church emphasised evangelism, diligent Scripture study — even developing the Antiochene School of biblical exegesis — and received the gifts of the Holy Spirit.

This extraordinary example is what we seek to follow. Vancouver's Antioch Church aims to share the gospel with the Chinese world, focus on expository preaching, and respect the work of the Holy Spirit.

我們的信仰What We Believe

信仰聲明Statement of Faith

Statement of Faith信仰聲明

我們，安提阿教會，全心全意接受上帝在新舊約聖經中的啟示，並以使徒信經、尼西亞信經和亞他那修信經為信仰的摘要。 We, Antioch Church, accept wholeheartedly the revelation of God in the Scriptures of the Old and New Testaments, summarised in the Apostles', Nicene, and Athanasian Creeds.

01
聖經，包括舊約和新約，是上帝所默示的話語，是祂旨意完整的啟示，是基督徒信仰生活各方面的最高權威。The Scripture, both Old and New Testament, to be the inspired Word of God, the complete revelation of His will, and the supreme authority for all Christian life.
02
三位一體的上帝，一神三位：聖父為創造者和保護者，聖子耶穌基督為救贖主和主，聖靈為重生者和成聖者。The triune God, one God in three persons: the Father as Creator, His Son Jesus Christ as Redeemer and Lord, and the Holy Spirit as Regenerator and Sanctifier.
03
全人類的罪性與罪債使人處於上帝公義的定罪之下。人雖按神的形象被造，卻因罪墮落，需要聖靈的重生方能得救。The universal sinfulness of humanity brings everyone under God's righteous condemnation. All people, created in God's image, fell into sin and require regeneration by the Holy Spirit for salvation.
04
因信基督而蒙上帝恩典稱義，是我們得救與救贖的唯一根據。Justification by the grace of God through faith in Christ, the incarnate and crucified Son of God, is the sole ground of our salvation and redemption.
05
聖靈的工作是光照、重生、內住並聖化信徒。The work of God the Holy Spirit is to illuminate, regenerate, indwell, and sanctify believers.
06
所有真信徒的合一構成一個普世的教會，基督是這個身體的頭。The unity of all true believers forms the one universal Church, the Body of which Christ is the Head.
07
我們相信並期待主耶穌基督親自、有形地再來。我們相信所有人的復活以接受最後審判，以及信徒的復活得以永遠與上帝同在。We believe and expect the personal, visible return of the Lord Jesus Christ, the resurrection of all people to final judgement, and the resurrection of believers to eternal life with God.
