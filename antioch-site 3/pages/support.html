為何奉獻 Why Give

我們是一個正在發展中的教會 A growing church community

Supported by the generosity of our community 依靠弟兄姐妹的慷慨奉獻

安提阿教會的一切事工——主日崇拜、講道、圣经与神学课程、3R心靈健康操、社群活動—— 都依靠弟兄姐妹的奉獻支持。

如果您因著上帝的感動，願意以奉獻支持我們的事奉，我們由衷地感謝。 如果您需要奉獻收據，請告知中文姓名與地址，我們會寄送給您。 Everything Antioch Church does — Sunday worship, preaching, training courses, the 3R Soul Fitness programme, community gatherings — is sustained by the generous giving of our congregation.

If God has moved you to support our ministry, we are deeply grateful. If you need a donation receipt, please provide your full legal name and address and we will send one to you.
