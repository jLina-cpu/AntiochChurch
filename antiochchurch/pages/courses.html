學習是信仰的一部分Learning as Part of Faith

Grow in Knowledge & Faith在知識與信仰中成長

安提阿教會重視聖經教導與神學培訓。我們相信，健康的信仰需要透過持續的查考聖經、神學反省與靈修操練來建立。以下課程資源，歡迎弟兄姐妹自由使用。 Antioch Church places great value on biblical teaching and theological formation. We believe healthy faith is built through ongoing Scripture study, theological reflection, and spiritual discipline. The following course resources are freely available to all.

📖
聖經研讀Bible Study
圣经与神学课程
系統性研讀舊約聖經各書卷，以正確的解經原理認識上帝的話語。此系列由白恩拾牧師主講，帶領弟兄姐妹逐書卷深度研讀舊約，涵蓋創世記、出埃及記、利未記、申命記，以及先知書、詩歌智慧文學等。 Systematic study of Old Testament books, guided by sound principles of biblical interpretation. Led by Pastor Victor Pai, this series takes participants through an in-depth study of the Old Testament — from Genesis and Exodus through the Prophets and Wisdom literature.

信仰基礎Christian Foundations
β-Course：基督教教義初探β-Course: Introduction to Christian Doctrine
系統性探索基督教核心教義，幫助信徒在信仰根基上更加穩固。共九課，涵蓋上帝觀、人觀、基督論、聖靈論、教會論、末世論、啟示論及教會歷史。 A systematic exploration of core Christian beliefs to help believers build a stronger faith foundation. Nine lessons covering the doctrines of God, humanity, Christ, the Holy Spirit, the Church, eschatology, revelation, and church history.
